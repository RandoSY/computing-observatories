BEFORE THE CLICK (v2 — Extended Classroom & Hardware Bridge Edition)
Five stations in interactive computing, 1962–1973 + Live Hardware Bridge
Randall Young's historical physical-computing track

1. Extract the entire ZIP.
2. Open Before_the_Click_Lab.html in Chrome or Edge. No server, internet, install,
   or account is required. Stations 01–04 need no hardware; Station 05 supports
   both built-in jitter simulation and live 3.3V/5V USB-UART microcontrollers.
3. Read Teacher_Guide.pdf and print one Student_Fieldbook.pdf per pair.
4. Use a mouse or trackpad for Stations 3 and 4 when available. Touch works.
   For keyboard input on those screens, focus the canvas and use arrows
   to move the pointer. Enter selects on the GT40. Hold Space and move to
   ink on the Alto; Enter toggles one pixel there.

WHAT'S NEW IN V2
- Live "Receive · Remember · Show" state triad bar on every station.
- Auditable LINC runs: prior trace stays visible in dimmed cyan rings alongside
  current bright-green points, with N and Δt (ms) explicitly displayed.
- Non-spoiling DSKY lamp test: post-test status reports completion without
  giving away the fault diagnosis before students compare against baseline.
- Closest-beam light-pen disambiguation and hex/binary byte inspection on Alto.
- Station 05 (Hardware Bridge): built-in jitter/packet-drop simulator plus
  WebSerial CSV ingestion (`seq,timestamp_us,adc_code`) for tabletop MCUs
  (MSP430, PIC, AVR, RP2040, BBC micro:bit).

Run in order: LINC, Apollo DSKY, DEC GT40, Xerox Alto, and optional Station 05.
The four core lessons take 45 minutes each; the transfer challenge takes ~30 minutes.
